Biography

I am a third-year Ph.D. student in Data Science at the New Jersey Institute of Technology (NJIT), jointly advised by Dr. Mengnan Du and Dr. Yingcong Li. My current research focuses on interpretability for large language models (LLMs). Before joining NJIT, I received my M.S. degree in Computer Science from Stevens Institute of Technology and my B.S. degree in Computer Science from China University of Geosciences, Wuhan.
